How to build

Parts snap to the stud grid and click onto whatever is underneath. Knock things over with the Throw tool. Your model saves itself in this browser.

Place a partClick. On a touch screen, tap to aim and tap again to place
Turn the partR, or Q for the other way. Works with any keyboard language
ThrowThrow tool (T): press and hold, then let go. Hold longer to throw harder
TNTBuild it in, then press D to light the fuses. A hard hit sets it off too, so try throwing it
Remove a partShift+click, right-click, or Remove tool (E). Anything resting on it falls
Copy a partAlt+click, or Copy tool (I)
PaintPaint tool (P), then click a part
Slow motionHold Space
Next part or color, and . for parts, [ and ] for colors
CameraDrag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom, F to reset
Touch screensTap to choose a spot, then tap the part again or press Place. The arrows move it one stud, and you can drag it to slide it. Pinch to zoom
Play togetherUp to 5 players. Tap Play together at the top: one player starts a game, and friends join with its code or an invite link from anywhere with internet. Or run play-together.bat to play over your own WiFi
Undo, redoCtrl+Z, Ctrl+Y. Undo also rebuilds what you knocked down
